# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First public release.

- Monthly revenue goal with round stages, an even-pace marker and profit alongside, set with `goal 7000`.
- One action for the week with a counter, set with `week 10 client offers`.
- Month review against the goal, carried by a clean start on the 1st.
- Growth view: comparison with last month and your record, milestone ladder that follows your goal, runway.
- Money view: net cash flow, six-month chart, budgets, recent transactions, with an all/business filter.
- One-line entry with a live preview and undo from the notification.
- Connect screen that verifies an API key before saving it.
- English and Russian.
